# Actual AI-assisted editorial workflow record

Project: Freedle & Associates editorial writing sample
Date: October 17, 2026
Owner and project director: Kim John Tuñacao
AI assistant: ChatGPT

## Request and approval
- User requested an in-depth researched article about Freedle & Associates for a writing-job application.
- User requested a fact-check of researched company information, including positive but potentially unverified claims.
- User approved the final editorial blueprint and directed production with a recorded workflow.
- User has **not** approved sending or publishing the work.

## Sources and searches
- Search categories: official company/team biographies, jobs, Southern Denver Living videos, relocation and seller content, public reviews, Apple Podcasts, competitor pages, Google SEO guidance, Colorado verification site, Fair Housing guidance.
- Representative search queries: See Section 02 of the separate Research, SEO and Workflow report.
- The source register in `Freedle_Evidence_Ledger.csv` identifies the 25 pages, evidence states, source limitations and check date.

## Key claim decisions
- Team roster: four professionals confirmed on current team directory.
- Jake/Megan are siblings: their own on-camera statement, not inferred from matching surnames.
- Scott describes working with his children: public profile, but private family details excluded.
- Lifetime sales/transactions: attributed to official bios, not independently proven.
- Jake top 1%: omitted because ranking issuer and period not established.
- Sara biography: search-index record sometimes returned direct-page error; corroborated professional community/gardening background with Honeywood Garden Design.
- Zillow count conflict: separate web captures displayed 86 reviews / 544 sales vs 87 reviews / 549 sales. Such mutable statistics excluded from the article.
- Positive reviews summarized as reviewers' experiences, not guarantees.
- No unsupported positive rumors or unverified negative allegations published.

## Editorial positioning
- Primary intent: branded informational search and consumer due diligence.
- Unique content angle: who the team members are and what questions customers should ask, rather than rewriting general Denver-neighborhood guides.
- Draft includes clearly sourced facts, optional service descriptions, and original neutral advice for interviewing an agent.
- No third-party or company imagery copied.

## Tools used
- ChatGPT: research orchestration, outline, claim-status decisions, drafting, rewriting and editorial quality checks.
- Public web search and source-page review: evidence discovery and corroboration.
- Python / python-docx: structured source ledger, reproducible DOCX production, candidate files and text QA.
- LibreOffice/PDF renderer: layout verification and PDF production.

## Accuracy and limitations
- We did not access employer accounts, paid SEO platforms, proprietary analytics, state broker record search results, MLS, client interview materials, or original company editorial approvals.
- We cannot claim search-volume targets, future rankings, conversion results, recent complete license status, or company endorsement.
- User review remains necessary before any external sharing.
